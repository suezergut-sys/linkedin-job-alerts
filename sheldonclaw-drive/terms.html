SheldonClawDRIVE · Last updated 26 October 2026

A personal synchronization and backup tool, not a public file-hosting service.

Private authorized use

The application is intended for its operator and explicitly authorized personal use. Only connect accounts and files you are entitled to access. This information site offers no public registration or access to private data.

Synchronization behavior

Configured synchronization propagates changes in both directions and can change, replace or delete files. Conflict preservation and version backups reduce some risks but cannot guarantee recovery from every failure. Keep independent, verified backups of valuable records and review any recovery or resynchronization plan before applying it.

Account control

Google authorization is voluntary and can be revoked in Google Account settings. The Privacy Policy describes access, storage and retention. Revocation does not itself erase existing private copies.

Availability and third parties

The tool depends on Google Drive, rclone, server hosting and network access. It does not guarantee uninterrupted service or a token that can never expire or be revoked. It is not an official Google or GitHub product; third-party services apply their own terms.

Support and future changes

Contact the operator through an existing private channel or the support email displayed on the Google consent screen. Any future expansion beyond the authorized personal use requires review of permissions, notices and applicable platform rules.
