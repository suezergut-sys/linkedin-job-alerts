SheldonClawDRIVE

Connects an authorized Google Drive account to the operator's private OpenClaw environment for Obsidian synchronization and independent backups.

Owner-controlled Google Drive access

What it does

The application uses rclone to synchronize a configured Obsidian folder between Google Drive and the operator's private server. It also uploads and verifies independent backup archives in designated backup folders.

Synchronization runs on a schedule. Changes can propagate in either direction, including authorized modifications and deletions; version backups and conflict handling preserve displaced copies where configured.

Why Google Drive access is requested

The OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive permits viewing, creating, changing and deleting Google Drive files. This broad permission is required to synchronize existing files and folders that were not created by the application.

Configured folder paths limit normal operation; they do not narrow the underlying Google permission. Access can be revoked in Google Account settings.

Information pages only. This website has no sign-in, vault browser or administration interface. It does not expose notes, file listings, credentials or backup archives.

Private use

This application is operated for its owner and explicitly authorized personal use. It is not a public storage service and does not offer public registration.

Google authorization does not make private files or the server publicly accessible.

Privacy and support

The Privacy Policy describes data access, storage and revocation. The Terms of Use explain the scope and limits of synchronization.

Authorized users may contact the operator through their existing private channel or the support email displayed on Google's consent screen.
