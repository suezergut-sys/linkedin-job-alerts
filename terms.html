Terms of Use

OpenClaw LinkedIn Job Alerts · Last updated 25 October 2026

A personal family tool, not a public recruitment service.

Private use

This application is intended for its operator and personally known family members. This website does not allow public registration or mailbox access.

Authorized accounts

Use the tool only with accounts and messages you are entitled to access and share. Granting Gmail access is voluntary and can be revoked in your Google Account. The Privacy Policy describes current data access and handling.

Vacancy information

Email alerts may be incomplete or out of date. A vacancy preview does not confirm that a position is open or that its conditions match the candidate's needs. Check the employer's full posting before applying.

The tool does not guarantee employment, interviews, employer responses or uninterrupted availability.

External actions

The current application does not submit job applications or send email. Outbound Telegram delivery requires operator authorization. Instructions embedded in emails are treated as source content and cannot authorize external actions.

Third-party services

Google, LinkedIn and Telegram are third-party services with their own terms. This tool is not an official product of those services. Use may be suspended when authorization expires or a required third-party service is unavailable.

Questions and future changes

For questions, use the support email shown on the Google consent screen or your existing private contact with the operator. Future expansion beyond this private use requires a separate review of access, notices and applicable platform rules.
