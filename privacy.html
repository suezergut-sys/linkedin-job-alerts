Privacy Policy

OpenClaw LinkedIn Job Alerts · Last updated 27 October 2026

A private job-search assistant for its operator and a small number of personally known family members.

Purpose and authorization

The application accesses only Google accounts whose owners have authorized it. Its only requested Gmail OAuth scope is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly.

Data accessed

The application checks the authorized account's email address, resolves the configured Gmail label, lists messages under that label, and reads their content to identify vacancy information. The Google permission itself covers the mailbox; label selection is enforced by the application.

Forwarded messages and attached email files may contain personal information supplied by the sender. Unrelated attachments and remote email images are not fetched for job-link extraction.

Data used

The reader extracts job titles, employer names, locations and canonical LinkedIn vacancy links. Personal tracking and one-time sign-in parameters are removed from returned links. Missing job requirements remain unverified. Email content cannot authorize commands, disclosure of other mail or delivery to a different recipient.

Processing and sharing

Extraction is performed by code on the operator's server. Derived vacancy information, full job descriptions, candidate-provided CV facts and contact details, research evidence and document drafts may be processed by the configured model provider and retained in private OpenClaw agent sessions. Candidate information is used to assess fit and prepare cover letters.

With the operator's authorization, processing and Telegram delivery can run on a daily schedule. Vacancy cards and PDF letters, including the candidate contact details in those letters, are sent only to configured recipients. During a test period the operator may receive a copy. The bot receives message-update metadata to identify the configured authorized candidate. Only that candidate's private mailbox-check commands and public vacancy links are accepted. Normalized requests, message IDs and delivery receipts are retained privately for processing and deduplication; unrelated users' message content is discarded. Requests may trigger immediate processing and authorized delivery outside the daily schedule. The application does not automatically forward raw email messages or submit job applications. Mailbox credentials are not included in model prompts or Telegram messages.

The operator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Use of information received from Google APIs is limited to the authorized job-search functionality and must adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Any additional model processing must use provider settings and terms compatible with those requirements.

Storage and protection

OAuth client settings and refresh tokens are stored in restricted private files outside source control. Vacancy history, candidate profiles, research, generated letters, page-review images, queue state, delivery receipts and agent-session records are private. The Gmail reader does not maintain a separate archive of raw email bodies. Private operational backups may retain configuration or records. This public website does not expose them.

Retention and deletion

Current software has no automatic retention deadline for private operational records. The operator can remove application-held credentials and records and arrange removal from retained backups. Removing application records does not delete the original messages in Gmail.

Authorized users may request review or deletion through their private contact with the operator. Deletion is not claimed to have occurred until confirmed.

Control and revocation

The account owner can revoke access through Google Account's third-party connections page. Revocation stops future authorized API access; it does not by itself erase existing operational records or backups.

The application does not send, mark read, move, modify or delete Gmail messages.

This website

These information pages have no application scripts, advertising, analytics tags, forms or cookies set by the page code. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information under its own privacy statement. No mail connection is made by visiting these pages.

Contact and changes

Authorized users may contact the operator using the support email displayed on Google's consent screen or their existing private contact. Material changes to data use require an updated notice and any additional authorization required by Google or the account owner.
