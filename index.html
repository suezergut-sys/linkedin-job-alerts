OpenClaw LinkedIn Job Alerts helps its operator and personally known family members turn email alerts into readable vacancy information.

Read-only Gmail access

What it does

The application reads LinkedIn job-alert emails under a designated Gmail label, including forwarded alerts. It keeps a private vacancy history, checks full descriptions and researches official employer sources.

Using a candidate-provided profile, it prepares cover letters in English, Finnish and Swedish as a PDF and sends authorized vacancy cards through Telegram.

Your account stays in control

Google authorization requests only gmail.readonly. The permission technically covers the mailbox; the application selects messages using its configured label.

It does not request permission to send, change or delete Gmail messages. Access can be revoked in your Google Account.

This is an information site. There is no public registration, mailbox browser or administration interface here. It does not expose emails, credentials or vacancy-delivery records.

Current scope

The operator can enable daily processing and scheduled delivery to configured family recipients. Unknown job conditions are labelled explicitly; confirmed mismatches are excluded. The application does not submit job applications or send email. A restricted private-chat command interface lets the configured candidate request a mailbox check or submit a public vacancy link; other chats and arbitrary commands are ignored.

AI-generated assessments and letters may contain mistakes. Check the employer's current requirements and review application documents before applying.

Privacy & support

Read the Privacy Policy for data access, processing, retention and revocation, and the Terms of Use for the scope of this personal tool.

Authorized users can contact the operator through their existing private contact channel or the support email shown on Google's consent screen.
